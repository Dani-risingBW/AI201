If you are unsure if you've used all of your federal subsidized and unsubsidized eligibility, please check your aggregate loan limit here: https://nsldsfap.ed.gov/ . For Graduate and Parent PLUS loans, please check www.studentaid.gov , to ensure the current PLUS loan has not expired (expires six (6) months after the first application submission). For additional inquiries, please reach out to finaid@howard.edu .
