If you have been granted a TPD discharge and the discharge was granted based on a physician’s certification or documentation from the Social Security Administration, the student is subject to a post-discharge monitoring period that starts on the date that the Department granted the discharge. During this period, the receipt of a new federal student aid or a subsequent disbursement that was initially received prior to the date that the Department of Education (ED) granted a discharge, may cause the student’s obligation to repay Direct Loans or the TEACH Grant service obligation to be reinstated. Note that if you received a TPD discharge because of documentation from the Veterans Administration (VA), then it is not subject to a post discharge monitoring period.
